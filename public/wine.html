Know your styles

what's in the glass.

A quick guide to the styles in our range: what they taste like, and when to pour them.

Sparkling and rosé

Brut NV
Fine bubbles, crisp apple and citrus, and a touch of toast. Non-vintage means it's blended across years for a consistent style.
Pour it for toasts, arrivals and announcements.
Rosé
Dry, pale and fresh, with strawberry and watermelon notes.
Pour it for summer events and long lunches.

White

Pinot Grigio
Light, crisp and refreshing, with pear and green apple.
Pour it for daytime events and seafood.
Sauvignon Blanc
Zesty and aromatic: passionfruit, lime and fresh-cut grass.
Pour it for warm weather, salads and seafood.
Riesling
Dry and bright, with lime, citrus blossom and a mineral edge. Ages beautifully.
Pour it with spicy and Asian dishes.
Chardonnay
From lean and citrusy to rich and creamy, depending on the region and the oak.
Pour it with chicken, pasta and creamy dishes.

Red

Pinot Noir
Light-bodied and silky, with cherry, raspberry and earthy notes.
Pour it with duck, mushrooms and salmon.
Grenache
Medium-bodied and juicy, with raspberry and spice.
Pour it with share plates and grilled meats.
Shiraz
Full and generous, with dark plum, blackberry and pepper.
Pour it at barbecues and winter dinners.
Cabernet Sauvignon
Structured and firm, with blackcurrant, mint and cedar.
Pour it with steak, lamb and cheese.

Wine regions

australia's wine regions.

Australia has 65 official wine regions, from the warm Barossa to cool Tasmania. The larger dots are the 33 you're most likely to see on a label. Choose any region to see its climate and what it's known for.

Best known Other regions

Western Australia

Margaret River

Climate Mild, maritime

  • Cabernet Sauvignon
  • Chardonnay
  • Semillon–Sauvignon Blanc

Surrounded on three sides by the Indian and Southern oceans, Margaret River's mild, even climate gives elegant, structured Cabernet and some of Australia's most celebrated Chardonnay.

Western Australia

Geographe

Climate Warm, maritime

  • Chardonnay
  • Shiraz
  • Cabernet Sauvignon

Just north of Margaret River, Geographe runs from the coast into the hills, with sea breezes tempering warm days for bright Chardonnay and medium-bodied reds.

Western Australia

Pemberton

Climate Cool, wetter

  • Chardonnay
  • Pinot Noir
  • Sauvignon Blanc

Among the tall karri forests of WA's south-west, Pemberton's cooler, wetter climate suits fine Chardonnay and Pinot Noir.

Western Australia

Great Southern

Climate Cool, maritime

  • Riesling
  • Shiraz
  • Cabernet Sauvignon

A large region on WA's south coast with five subregions, from Frankland River to Albany. Known for taut, citrusy Riesling and peppery, cool-climate Shiraz.

Western Australia

Swan District

Climate Hot, dry

  • Chenin Blanc
  • Verdelho
  • Fortified

Western Australia's oldest wine region, including the Swan Valley on Perth's doorstep. Hot summers suit generous whites like Chenin Blanc and Verdelho, and rich fortified wines.

South Australia

Clare Valley

Climate Warm days, cool nights

  • Riesling
  • Shiraz
  • Cabernet Sauvignon

Clare's warm days and cold nights give bone-dry, lime-scented Riesling that ages beautifully, along with firm, savoury Shiraz and Cabernet.

South Australia

Barossa Valley

Climate Warm, dry

  • Shiraz
  • Grenache
  • Mataro (Mourvèdre)

Home to some of the world's oldest Shiraz vines, the Barossa makes rich, generous reds full of dark fruit and spice, and old-vine Grenache and Mataro.

South Australia

Eden Valley

Climate Cool, elevated

  • Riesling
  • Shiraz
  • Chardonnay

Higher and cooler than its Barossa neighbour, Eden Valley is famous for fine, floral Riesling and elegant, perfumed Shiraz.

South Australia

Adelaide Hills

Climate Cool, elevated

  • Sauvignon Blanc
  • Chardonnay
  • Pinot Noir

In the Mount Lofty Ranges above Adelaide, altitude keeps things cool: crisp Sauvignon Blanc, fine Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and sparkling.

South Australia

McLaren Vale

Climate Mediterranean, maritime

  • Shiraz
  • Grenache
  • Cabernet Sauvignon

Between the Mount Lofty Ranges and the sea, McLaren Vale's Mediterranean climate gives ripe, generous Shiraz with a savoury edge, and celebrated old-vine Grenache.

South Australia

Langhorne Creek

Climate Moderate, lake breezes

  • Cabernet Sauvignon
  • Shiraz
  • Malbec

Cooled by breezes off Lake Alexandrina, Langhorne Creek makes soft, generous reds, and its grapes find their way into many well-known blends.

South Australia

Riverland

Climate Hot, inland

  • Shiraz
  • Chardonnay
  • Alternative varieties

Irrigated by the Murray River, the Riverland is one of Australia's biggest producing regions, and a growing home for Mediterranean and alternative varieties.

South Australia

Coonawarra

Climate Cool, maritime

  • Cabernet Sauvignon
  • Shiraz

A narrow strip of red terra rossa soil over limestone gives Coonawarra its famous Cabernet Sauvignon: blackcurrant, mint and fine, firm tannins.

Victoria

Grampians

Climate Cool, continental

  • Shiraz
  • Sparkling
  • Riesling

Around Great Western, the Grampians has a long history of sparkling wine, including sparkling Shiraz, and makes peppery, medium-bodied still Shiraz.

Victoria

Heathcote

Climate Warm, continental

  • Shiraz

Deep red Cambrian soils, formed more than 500 million years ago, give Heathcote's Shiraz its depth, structure and dark, plummy fruit.

Victoria

Goulburn Valley

Climate Warm

  • Shiraz
  • Marsanne
  • Cabernet Sauvignon

Along the Goulburn River, including the Nagambie Lakes subregion, known for structured Shiraz and long-lived Marsanne.

Victoria

Macedon Ranges

Climate Very cool, elevated

  • Sparkling
  • Chardonnay
  • Pinot Noir

One of mainland Australia's coolest wine regions, just north-west of Melbourne. Ideal for fine sparkling, taut Chardonnay and Pinot Noir.

Victoria

Geelong

Climate Cool, maritime

  • Pinot Noir
  • Chardonnay
  • Shiraz

Around Port Phillip and the Bellarine Peninsula, Geelong's cool, windy climate makes intense Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and spicy Shiraz.

Victoria

Yarra Valley

Climate Cool

  • Chardonnay
  • Pinot Noir
  • Sparkling
  • Cabernet Sauvignon

Victoria's first wine region, an hour from Melbourne. Elegant Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, fine sparkling, and medium-bodied Cabernet and Shiraz.

Victoria

Mornington Peninsula

Climate Cool, maritime

  • Pinot Noir
  • Chardonnay
  • Pinot Gris

Surrounded by sea on three sides, the Peninsula's cool maritime climate is made for Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and textural Pinot Gris.

Victoria

Gippsland

Climate Cool, maritime

  • Pinot Noir
  • Chardonnay

A large, sparsely planted region east of Melbourne, with small producers making characterful Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.

Victoria

King Valley

Climate Cool, alpine foothills

  • Prosecco
  • Pinot Grigio
  • Sangiovese
  • Nebbiolo

Settled by Italian families, the King Valley is the home of Australian Prosecco and a hub for Italian varieties like Pinot Grigio, Sangiovese and Nebbiolo.

Victoria

Beechworth

Climate Cool to moderate, elevated

  • Chardonnay
  • Shiraz

A small, high region in north-east Victoria with a big reputation for powerful, complex Chardonnay and Shiraz.

Victoria

Rutherglen

Climate Hot, continental

  • Fortified Muscat and Topaque
  • Durif
  • Shiraz

Famous for its world-class fortified Muscat and Topaque, aged for decades, and big, inky reds from Durif and Shiraz.

New South Wales & ACT

Riverina

Climate Hot, inland

  • Botrytis Semillon
  • Shiraz
  • Chardonnay

Around Griffith, irrigated vineyards make a large share of Australia's everyday wine, and the region is famous for luscious botrytis Semillon dessert wine.

New South Wales & ACT

Tumbarumba

Climate Cool, high altitude

  • Chardonnay
  • Pinot Noir
  • Sparkling

In the foothills of the Snowy Mountains, Tumbarumba's high, cool vineyards give fine Chardonnay and base wine for top sparkling.

New South Wales & ACT

Canberra District

Climate Cool, continental

  • Shiraz (often with Viognier)
  • Riesling

Spanning the ACT and southern NSW, the Canberra District is known for fragrant Shiraz, often co-fermented with a little Viognier, and fine Riesling.

New South Wales & ACT

Hilltops

Climate Continental, elevated

  • Shiraz
  • Cabernet Sauvignon

Around Young, Hilltops' elevated vineyards and warm days give deeply coloured, full-flavoured Shiraz and Cabernet.

New South Wales & ACT

Orange

Climate Cool, high altitude

  • Chardonnay
  • Sauvignon Blanc
  • Pinot Noir
  • Shiraz

Defined by altitude, with vineyards above 600 metres on the slopes of Mount Canobolas. Crisp whites, Pinot Noir and cool-climate Shiraz.

New South Wales & ACT

Mudgee

Climate Warm, continental

  • Shiraz
  • Cabernet Sauvignon
  • Chardonnay

On the western slopes of the Great Dividing Range, Mudgee makes generous, full-bodied reds and rich Chardonnay.

New South Wales & ACT

Hunter Valley

Climate Warm, humid

  • Semillon
  • Shiraz
  • Chardonnay

One of Australia's oldest wine regions. Hunter Semillon is picked early, light in alcohol and ages for decades; Hunter Shiraz is medium-bodied and earthy.

Queensland

Granite Belt

Climate Cool, high altitude

  • Shiraz
  • Cabernet Sauvignon
  • Alternative varieties

Queensland's high-country wine region, with vineyards at altitude on granite soils, and a reputation for alternative 'Strange Bird' varieties.

Tasmania

Tasmania

Climate Cool, maritime

  • Sparkling
  • Pinot Noir
  • Chardonnay
  • Riesling

Australia's coolest wine state, from the Tamar Valley in the north to the Coal River and Huon valleys in the south. World-class sparkling, Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Riesling.

Western Australia

Perth Hills

Climate Warm, elevated

  • Shiraz
  • Cabernet Sauvignon
  • Chardonnay

In the Darling Range east of Perth, where altitude takes the edge off the heat. Small producers make Shiraz, Cabernet and Chardonnay.

Western Australia

Peel

Climate Warm, Mediterranean

  • Shiraz
  • Chenin Blanc

A small region south of Perth around Mandurah and the Peel Inlet, with warm weather suited to Shiraz and Chenin Blanc.

Western Australia

Blackwood Valley

Climate Warm, continental

  • Shiraz
  • Cabernet Sauvignon
  • Chardonnay

Inland from Margaret River around Bridgetown and Boyup Brook, with warm days and cool nights for Shiraz, Cabernet and Chardonnay.

Western Australia

Manjimup

Climate Cool, inland

  • Chardonnay
  • Pinot Noir
  • Merlot

Among the karri and jarrah forests of the south-west, Manjimup's cool climate suits Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Merlot.

South Australia

Southern Flinders Ranges

Climate Warm, elevated

  • Shiraz
  • Cabernet Sauvignon

North of the Clare Valley in the foothills of the Flinders Ranges, making full-flavoured Shiraz and Cabernet.

South Australia

Adelaide Plains

Climate Hot, dry

  • Shiraz
  • Cabernet Sauvignon
  • Chardonnay

On the flat, warm plains north of Adelaide, making ripe, generous reds and whites.

South Australia

Kangaroo Island

  • Shiraz

Surrounded by the Southern Ocean, the island's maritime climate gives fresh, elegant wines from a small number of producers.

South Australia

South of McLaren Vale, cooled by breezes from two coastlines, making fresh whites and elegant Shiraz.

South Australia

Near Goolwa and the mouth of the Murray, with sea and lake breezes moderating the warmth.

South Australia

  • Chardonnay
  • Shiraz

On the Limestone Coast, long a source of quality Chardonnay and Shiraz for both blends and estate wines.

South Australia

Mount Benson

South Australia

South Australia

how we choose it.

Every wine is described by what's in the bottle: the grape, where it's from and the year.

Direct from the vineyard
Buying direct keeps the price fair for the quality in the bottle.
Tasted before we offer it
If we wouldn't pour it for our own guests, it isn't in the range.
Backing Australian growers
Every case puts money in the hands of an Australian vineyard doing the work properly.
750 mL bottles
Packed and delivered in cases of 12, one wine per case.
One wine per case

Every case is 12 bottles of the same wine, so we don't mix wines in a case. Want a red and a white? Order a case of each, and delivery to capital cities and the Gold Coast is on us.
