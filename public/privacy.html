ONIV Pty Ltd

privacy policy.

Last updated 14 November 2026.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry or place an order, we collect the details you give us: your name, email, phone number, business name, delivery address, the occasion, any logo or artwork you upload, and anything else you include in your message.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare your label mockup.
  • To quote, take payment for and fulfil your order.
  • To arrange delivery, including confirming the recipient is 18 or over.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including liquor licensing and tax records.

Who we share it with

We share only what's needed to fulfil your order: the delivery details with our courier, and payment details with our payment provider. Our website and email providers store enquiries for us. We don't sell your information or use it for anyone else's marketing.

Your logo and artwork

We use your logo or artwork only to design and print your labels. We won't show your label publicly, such as on our website or social media, without your permission.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping it safe

We keep your information secure and only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires.

Access and correction

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing alex@oniv.com.au or calling 0475 799 070. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, tell us and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
