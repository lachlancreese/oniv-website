FAQ

good questions.

Can't see yours? Call 0475 799 070 or email alex@oniv.com.au.

What's the minimum order?
One case of 12 bottles of the same wine. Order one for a dinner, or one for every client on your list.
Can I mix wines in a case?
No. We only sell by the case, and every case is 12 bottles of one wine, labelled and packed together. Want a red and a white? Order a case of each, and delivery to capital cities is on us.
How much does it cost?
From $24 a bottle, including GST, your label design, printing and application. Your quote depends on the wine you choose and how many cases. See pricing
Do I need a logo or a designer?
No. Send a logo if you have one. If not, we set your name or words in type and design the label with you.
How long does it take?
Your mockup takes 1–2 business days. Once you approve it, in-stock wines are dispatched within 2 business days and delivery takes 5–7 business days. Allow 14 business days at most from approval.
Can I have it for Christmas?
Approve your label by 14 December and we dispatch by 16 December, ahead of the Christmas delivery cut-offs for metro areas. For WA and NT, approve by 3 December. Regional addresses need extra time.
What wine is it?
Australian wine we have tasted and chosen ourselves, from sparkling to Shiraz. We send the current range, with tasting notes, alongside your mockup. About the wine
Where do you deliver?
Delivery is on us for two or more cases to every capital city and the Gold Coast. A single case is $35 to deliver. For regional addresses, we'll include delivery in your quote.
Can I change the design?
As often as you like. We keep refining your proof until you love it, and nothing prints until you approve it.
Do I need to be 18?
Yes. You must be 18 or over to order, and someone 18 or over needs to receive the delivery.
